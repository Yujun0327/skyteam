# Rulings

One line each; every ruling has a test.

- RL-1 RNG order: traffic dice are drawn first inside the first player's `roll`, then that player's four faces in index order; the second `roll` draws four; a reroll draws one per masked die in index order. Faces never travel on the wire.
- RL-2 Brakes: final-round check is `speed ≤ brake` with brake ∈ {0,2,4,6} and brake ≥ 2 required (BGA).
- RL-3 Wind rotates the ring by the axis value immediately after the second Axis die, before any Engine resolution in the same round.
- RL-4 A die may be discarded only when it has no legal placement even with every coffee option.
- RL-5 An intern token taken this round must be placed this round; it cannot take coffee and cannot go on Concentration; an unplaced token at the end of the round is returned to the front of the board (game continues).
- RL-6 Re-placing on a green landing-gear or flap slot is legal and does nothing.
- RL-7 A Radio die with no plane at its target is legal and does nothing.
- RL-8 Real-time `timeout` may be submitted only by the turn holder.
- RL-9 Working Together swaps one own unplaced die with one partner unplaced die, once per round; Adaptation flips to `7 − value`, once per game per player; Anticipation is a single-die reroll by the first player before their first placement.
- RL-10 Advance procedure per step: Turns check on the space being left, then collision on the space being left, then move; past the airport = overshoot. Pass-through spaces are checked, the destination is not (BGA).
- RL-11 Holding pattern: at the airport before the final round, any advance > 0 is an overshoot.
- RL-12 Coffee is capped at 3; placing on Concentration when holding 3 still consumes the die.
- RL-13 Engine loss (TER): the Engine slots do not exist, each player places exactly 3 of their 4 dice, and the plane advances 1 at the end of every round through the normal advance procedure; the final-round speed check is skipped.
- RL-14 Synchronisation fires once per round, at the moment the second of (any gear, any flap) receives a die; the traffic die is placed by the Co-Pilot immediately as an extra action of the current turn.
